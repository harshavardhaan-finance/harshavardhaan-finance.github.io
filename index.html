About Me

I am a Qualified Cost and Management Accountant (CMA) with a B.Com background and experience across accounting, financial reporting, taxation, cost audit and statutory compliance.

My work has included preparing financial statements, inventory valuation and job costing, supporting tax planning and filings, and working with clients, banks and regulators to resolve financial and compliance queries.

Alongside this, I have built practical skills in Excel, SQL, Power BI, financial modelling and basic AI tools through hands-on projects in FP&A, budgeting and forecasting, variance analysis, working capital, valuation and corporate finance.
