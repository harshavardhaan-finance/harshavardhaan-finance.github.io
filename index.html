I'm a qualified Cost and Management Accountant (CMA, Aug 2026) and a B.Com graduate. I have 15 months of practical experience from my CMA practical training at a cost accountants' firm and a season at a tax filing company.

In training I assisted on cost audits of manufacturing clients and walked their production floors, prepared cash credit statements and loan projections for bank clients, finalised accounts for 20+ clients in Tally, and handled income tax and GST work.

I prepared for CMA Final on my own without coaching. Alongside it I built the projects on this site to get better at MS Excel, Power BI, SQL and AI tools, across costing, FP&A, treasury, valuation and project finance.

I'm now looking to start my career in a costing, finance or financial analysis team. I'm open to relocation.
