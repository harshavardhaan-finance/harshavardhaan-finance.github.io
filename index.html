About Me

A qualified CMA who likes finding out what things really cost, and what that means for the decision.

CMA QualifiedB.ComExcelSQLPower BI Financial ModellingBasic AI ToolsLearning: Python, ML & Automation

I am a Qualified Cost and Management Accountant (CMA, Aug 2026) with a B.Com from Coimbatore. I did my 15-month articleship at a cost accountants' firm, where I finalised accounts for 20+ clients, assisted on cost audits (CRA-2, CRA-4), visited production floors and prepared loan projections for bank borrowers.

I learn best by doing, so alongside CMA Final I kept practising on case studies. I build cost sheets of listed companies from their annual reports and reconcile them to the reported numbers, and I work through budgeting, cash flow, project appraisal and valuation cases in Excel, Power BI and SQL.

I use AI assistants for research, data checks and drafting, and every project page says what was mine and what was assisted. Projects built on a real company's public data are marked Real company; the rest are practice cases.

Currently Learning

  • Advanced finance: building on my CMA foundation with deeper finance concepts, applied through hands-on portfolio projects
  • Python & machine learning basics: Python fundamentals and everyday scripting, with ML explored through simple credit-risk scoring ideas ("Finsta QuickLoans" and "Debtor Intelligence" in Builds)
  • AI-based finance micro SaaS: exploring small, focused AI tools that solve specific finance and costing problems
  • AI automation & agentic tools: using AI to research, structure workflows and speed up repetitive tasks
  • Low-code / no-code app building ("vibe coding"): small working experiments on platforms like Google AI Studio, without deep coding expertise yet

All of this is early-stage and self-taught. I'm not proficient yet; I'm building the habit of learning by doing. The actual experiments are in the Builds section.

I am looking for a role in costing or corporate finance, where analysis feeds real business decisions. I am open to relocating within India or abroad.
